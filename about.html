Quality Assurance

Seven checks before an email is called valid.

Finding an address is trivial. Proving the recipient is reachable, active, and compliant is where our engineering shines.

Step 1 of 7

Syntax & Formatting Validation

Every email string is inspected for syntax conformity, typographical slips, illegal ASCII characters, and standard domain formatting rules.

Step 2 of 7

Domain Health & DNS Check

We test whether the company domain is active, properly registered, and possesses valid DNS records rather than parked or expiring domains.

Step 3 of 7

Mail Exchange (MX) Server Test

Our engines locate and ping the target mail exchange servers to ensure the recipient infrastructure is accepting inbound email traffic.

Step 4 of 7

Direct Mailbox Response Ping

Without delivering spam or disturbing the user, we perform deep SMTP handshake checks to confirm the specific user inbox exists and is open.

Step 5 of 7

Catch-All Domain Detection

Catch-all servers accept all sent emails regardless of validity. We cross-reference historical reply signals to verify whether the individual is authentic.

Step 6 of 7

Historical Bounce Risk Scoring

We apply AI classification models across past delivery data to flag dormancy risks or recent executive departures.

Step 7 of 7

Clean CRM File Preparation

Duplicates are merged, suppression lists applied, firmographics enriched, and the clean dataset is exported in your desired schema.
