Refund & Replacement Policy

Digital Goods Policy: Because verified business records are immediately downloadable, all delivered data files are generally non-refundable once accessed. However, our deliverability guarantee protects your purchase.

1. Hard-Bounce Guarantee

We guarantee at least 95% deliverability on all verified email lists. If your campaign encounters hard bounces exceeding 5% within 7 days of delivery (e.g. unknown user, mailbox non-existent), we provide an immediate 1:1 verified replacement or pro-rata credit.

2. How to Request Replacements

Simply export the bounce logs from your sending platform (e.g. HubSpot, Apollo, Mailchimp) and email them to support@b2bdatallc.com. Our data hygiene team will review the log within 24 hours.
