Privacy Policy

At B2B DATA GLOBAL LLC, we respect the privacy of individuals and businesses. This policy sets out how we source, process, and safeguard business-to-business information.

1. Sourcing and Lawful Basis

We process B2B business contact records based on legitimate interest under GDPR, CCPA, and relevant data protection laws. Data is compiled from publicly accessible registrations, corporate team directories, and professional opt-in contributor networks.

2. Opt-Out and Right to Erasure

Any professional has the absolute right to inspect or permanently remove their business profile from our database. To request immediate removal, contact privacy@b2bdatallc.com. Removal requests are enacted within 48 hours across all active master repositories.

3. Data Security

We implement SOC 2 aligned organizational and technical controls to guard against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration of digital assets.
